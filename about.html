More Than a Manufacturer.
A Partner Behind Your Brand.

At TN28 Millets, we believe every great food brand starts with an idea — but ideas alone don't reach the shelf.

Getting there takes product expertise, dependable processes, manufacturing capability, and unwavering consistency — the parts of the journey that go unseen but decide whether a brand succeeds.

We work with businesses who want to develop and manufacture food products without the cost, time, or risk of building their own manufacturing infrastructure.

As your B2B food manufacturing partner, we work behind the scenes — turning product requirements into commercially viable, market-ready food products, while you focus on building your brand and reaching your customers.

Your brand remains yours.
Your vision remains yours.

Our Mission

To give food businesses dependable manufacturing capability — the production capacity, quality systems, and technical expertise to turn good food ideas into consistent, market-ready products. We combine certified manufacturing processes, disciplined production standards, and hands-on formulation support to help businesses build and scale their product portfolios with confidence.

Our Vision

To be the certified, trusted manufacturing capability behind the next generation of Indian food brands. We envision a future where food entrepreneurs and established businesses alike can access dependable production expertise, develop meaningful products, and scale their ideas — backed by manufacturing they can rely on.
