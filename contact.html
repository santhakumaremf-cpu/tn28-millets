Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer private-label and white-label manufacturing?

Yes. We support customized food manufacturing based on product, ingredients, packaging and order quantity.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For customized products, minimum order quantity is typically 100 kg and above. Pricing varies by product and customization.

How long does product development take?

Depending on complexity and approvals, customization usually takes approximately 20–30 working days.

Can we order samples?

Yes. Sample packs are available. Retail packs from 500 g and bulk from 5 kg onwards for existing products.

What certifications do you have?

FSSAI, ISO 22000:2018, GMP and related quality documents are available.
